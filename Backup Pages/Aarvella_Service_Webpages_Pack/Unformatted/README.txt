AARVELLA SERVICE PAGE PACK

Files included:
- services.html: service directory with links to all category pages
- hair.html: includes separate Ladies Hair and Gentlemen's Grooming sections
- beauty.html: facials, cleanups, waxing, threading, bleach and beauty essentials
- spa.html: spa, manicure, pedicure and body rituals
- makeup.html: occasion, HD, airbrush, groom and bridal makeup
- styles.css: shared Aarvella black-gold premium styling
- app.js: mobile menu + booking popup interactions

Installation:
1. Upload all files to your website root folder.
2. If you already have services.html, backup your old file first.
3. Link your existing index.html navigation to services.html, hair.html, beauty.html, spa.html and makeup.html as needed.
4. Replace Unsplash image URLs with your original Aarvella salon images before final launch.
5. Connect the booking popup buttons to your CRM/booking widget.
