Before your appointment

Hair service questions, answered.

For colour corrections, major transformations or chemical texture services, share your recent hair history when booking.

Do I need a consultation before colour or texture services?

Yes. A consultation helps us understand previous colour or chemical services, assess the current condition and recommend a safe path towards the result you want.

Can the stylist help me choose a haircut?

Absolutely. Your stylist will consider face shape, natural texture, density, lifestyle and styling habits before recommending a shape and finish.

Should I wash my hair before my appointment?

For most services, arrive with hair in its usual condition unless the salon team gives you specific preparation instructions while confirming the appointment.

How do I know which dedicated service page to use?

Use Women’s Hair or Men’s Hair for cuts and styling, Hair Colour for shade services, and Hair Texture for smoothening, perming, spa and repair treatments.
