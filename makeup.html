01

Engagement Makeup

Your first signature celebration look.

Engagement makeup sits between effortless occasion beauty and full bridal intensity. We shape the look around the ceremony, photography, outfit and whether you prefer soft romance, modern definition or a more glamorous finish.

  • Personalised skin preparation and complexion finish
  • Eye detailing suited to the outfit and event lighting
  • Lashes and long-wear finishing where appropriate
  • Coordination with hairstyle and draping requirements
02

Family & Friends Occasions

Polished enough for photographs. Comfortable enough for the celebration.

For mothers, sisters, bridesmaids, friends and guests, the look should complement the occasion without competing with it. We create refined makeup that photographs beautifully and remains comfortable through a long event.

  • Soft glam, classic or defined occasion looks
  • Age-aware makeup for mature skin
  • Looks coordinated for groups and family members
  • Suitable for weddings, receptions, cocktails and celebrations
03

Groom Makeup

Fresh, composed and unmistakably natural.

Groom makeup is subtle correction, not visible coverage. The focus is even-looking skin, controlled shine, a rested appearance and grooming that holds up under high-resolution photography and long ceremonies.

  • Skin preparation and natural complexion correction
  • Under-eye, redness, blemish and beard-shadow balancing
  • Shine control for camera and stage lighting
  • Hair and beard finish coordination
04

Portfolio Makeup

Designed for the lens and the story behind it.

Portfolio makeup is planned with the photographer, concept, wardrobe and lighting in mind. It may be natural and commercially polished, fashion-led, editorial or built around a distinctive visual identity.

  • Model and actor portfolio looks
  • Personal branding and professional headshots
  • Editorial, fashion and beauty concepts
  • Social content, campaigns and creative shoots

Before the makeup chair

Makeup service questions, answered.

Share your event timing, location, outfit and hairstyle requirements while booking so the team can plan the appointment correctly.

How should I prepare for my makeup appointment?

Arrive with a clean face unless instructed otherwise. Share reference images and your outfit in advance, and tell the artist about allergies, sensitivities or current skin concerns.

Do you offer makeup for mothers, sisters and wedding guests?

Yes. Family and friends occasion makeup can be planned for individuals or groups, including age-aware looks for mature skin and coordinated finishes for wedding parties.

Will groom makeup look obvious?

No. Groom makeup is designed to look natural. It focuses on subtle correction, controlled shine, a rested appearance and a camera-ready finish.

What should I share for a portfolio makeup booking?

Share the shoot concept, photographer, lighting setup, wardrobe, intended usage and visual references so the artist can plan the finish and level of detail.
