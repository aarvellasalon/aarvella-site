Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Aarvella Unisex Salon ("Aarvella", "we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares and protects personal information when you visit aarvella.com, book an appointment, create a customer account, use the AI Stylist tool, or visit our salon in Dehradun.

This policy is drafted to align with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA) and applicable Indian information-technology rules. It applies to our website, our customer portal, and our booking system. It does not apply to third-party websites you may reach through links on our site.

Information We Collect

Account and booking information. When you sign in or register through our customer portal (powered by Auth0) or book an appointment, we collect your name, email address, phone number, and — if you choose to provide them — gender, date of birth, profile photo, and postal address.

Appointment and visit history. We keep a record of the services you book, the branch and stylist involved, appointment dates and times, and status (booked, completed, cancelled, no-show). This history is what lets our team and our systems recognise you as a returning customer.

Beauty and wellness profile. If you choose to complete a profile in the customer portal, we may store hair type, hair texture and length, scalp concerns, chemical treatment history, skin type and concerns, known allergies and product sensitivities, preferred styles, and any goals or notes you add. This information is entirely optional, is provided at your discretion, and directly affects the services and product choices our stylists make for you — please see "Service Consultations, Patch Tests and Allergies" in our Terms of Service for how we rely on this information.

Consultation and staff notes. Our stylists and reception staff may record notes about your visits or preferences to help us serve you consistently. Some notes are marked staff-only for internal service-quality purposes and are not shown in your customer portal.

Communications. If you contact us by phone, WhatsApp, or the AI Stylist chat widget, we may retain a record of that conversation to respond to you and improve our service.

Technical and usage information. Our website is served through Cloudflare, which processes standard web request data (such as IP address, browser type, and request timestamps) as part of routine security and performance operations. We use functional cookies only — a session cookie to keep you signed in, and a CSRF token to protect portal forms from cross-site attacks. We do not use third-party advertising or analytics cookies.

Newsletter. If you subscribe using the footer form, we store your email address for the purpose of sending you salon updates.

How We Use Information

  • To create and manage your customer account and authenticate you securely.
  • To schedule, confirm, modify and remind you about appointments, including by phone, email, SMS and WhatsApp.
  • To help our stylists deliver the right service safely — in particular, to check your disclosed allergies and sensitivities before recommending products or chemical treatments.
  • To operate our loyalty and rewards program, where applicable.
  • To respond to enquiries made through our website, WhatsApp, or the AI Stylist chat widget.
  • To send you salon updates and offers where you have opted in, and to let you opt out at any time.
  • To maintain the security, integrity and proper functioning of our website and portal.
  • To comply with our legal, accounting and tax obligations.

Legal Basis For Processing

We process your personal data on the basis of your consent (for example, when you create an account, complete your beauty profile, or opt in to marketing messages), for the performance of the service you have requested (booking and delivering a salon appointment), and to comply with our legal obligations (such as financial record-keeping). Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time as described in "Your Rights" below, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

Cookies

Our customer portal sets two functional cookies: a session cookie (to keep you signed in, marked HttpOnly and sent only over HTTPS in production) and a CSRF token (to protect you from cross-site request forgery on portal forms). Cloudflare, our security and content-delivery provider, may also set its own cookies as part of standard bot-protection and performance services. We do not currently use any advertising, marketing, or third-party analytics cookies on this site.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only as follows:

  • Auth0 — our authentication provider, which manages your login credentials and identity verification.
  • Our booking and appointment system — operated by Aarvella, which stores your appointment and service history so our reception and stylists can serve you.
  • Cloudflare — our content delivery and security provider, which sits in front of our website for performance and protection against abuse.
  • Our AI Stylist feature — see the dedicated section below.
  • WhatsApp — if you message us or we message you via WhatsApp, that conversation is subject to WhatsApp's own privacy practices as well as ours.
  • Legal and safety disclosures — where required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, property or safety of Aarvella, our staff, or our customers.

We may in future engage additional service providers — for example, for email/SMS delivery or payment processing. If we do, we will update this policy before that provider begins processing your data.

The AI Stylist Feature

Our website includes an optional AI Stylist chat widget. Messages you type into this widget are sent to OpenAI (via a backend function we control) to generate a response; our API credentials are never exposed to your browser. Do not share sensitive personal information (such as medical details, financial information, or government-issued ID numbers) in this chat — it is intended for style inspiration only, not as a record of your account or appointment. See our Terms of Service for the AI Stylist disclaimer.

Data Retention

We retain your account and appointment information for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward to meet our accounting, tax, warranty and dispute-resolution obligations. If you request anonymisation of your account (see "Your Rights"), we remove or irreversibly de-identify personal identifiers while retaining the underlying transaction records we are legally required to keep.

Data Security

We use industry-standard safeguards appropriate to a business of our size, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed/secret credentials that are never stored in our source code, parameterised database queries to prevent injection attacks, and access controls that separate customer-visible data from internal staff notes. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your information and to respond promptly to any incident.

Your Rights

As a data principal under Indian law, and directly from your Aarvella customer portal (Settings), you can:

  • Access and export your data — submit a data export request from the Settings page, and we will provide a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct your information — update your profile, preferences and beauty profile at any time from the portal.
  • Request account anonymisation — submit an anonymisation request from the Settings page. We will remove or de-identify your personal identifiers, subject to records we must retain by law.
  • Withdraw consent — turn off marketing, reminder, AI-personalisation or before/after-photo consents individually from the portal at any time.
  • Raise a grievance — contact our Grievance Officer using the details below if you are unsatisfied with how we have handled your personal data.

Children's Privacy

Our customer portal and online account features are intended for individuals aged 18 and above. We welcome younger clients for salon services when accompanied by a parent or guardian, but a parent or guardian must create and manage any online account or booking on their behalf.
