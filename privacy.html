Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Aarvella collects, uses and protects basic customer information shared during enquiries, bookings, consultations, newsletter signups and salon visits.

Information We Collect

We may collect your name, phone number, email address, service preferences, appointment details and any voluntary information you share for consultation or follow-up.

How We Use Information

We use information to manage bookings, confirm appointments, improve service quality, send relevant updates and maintain customer communication.

Data Care

We aim to keep your information secure, limited and used only for legitimate salon operations. Do not share sensitive personal information unless required for a specific service consultation.

Contact

For privacy questions or update requests, contact Aarvella Salon through the official phone number or booking channels listed on this website.
